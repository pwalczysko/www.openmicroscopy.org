OMERO Workshop Setup

All our training preparation materials are available to help you set up and run your own OMERO workshop.

What do I need to organize an OMERO workshop?

Although the OME Team is giving workshops using their own resources, there might be many situations where you will or should prefer running your own workshop instead. These might be situations where your setup needs to be very specific, very demanding, your users insist on the training server being available all the time or for long periods of time, or, simply, you need more freedom in timing and frequency of the OMERO workshops at your institution.

  • OMERO server is a must. You can either use your own production server or set up a dedicated training server on your site. Deploy the server preferably using Ansible, or, use the Ansible playbook to deploy it manually.
  • OMERO Apps to give your users full experience, the vanilla OMERO server will not do. Make sure you have all the Apps installed according to the playbook [LINK TO APPs]. If you need to know what the Apps are doing, consult [LINK to guides, link to Apps www page, link to pypi].
  • Image data on the server are very useful. It can be argued that without a good portfolio of image data your workshop will not be able to show many OMERO features at all. Relying on your users to provide such data during your workshop is not realistic. Retrieve the data from [LINK to images] and import them to your server. You can use the [Link to import script]. The data can be preimported for a single user or several users on your OMERO server, but import for as many users as possible to minimize the confusion during the workshop, in case users are using or accessing the data of someone else. Like this, you will be able to show both the work on your own data in OMERO but also the cooperation aspects of OMERO.
  • Metadata/Annotations/Result tables are needed for similar reasons as the image data. Use [LINK to Scripts] to populate the secondary metadata in your OMERO server. Use [LINK to OMERO.tables and CSV with results] to get the analysis results talbes for the imported images. Some features, such as data management, OMERO.parade and OMERO.figure cannot be shown in a meaningful manner without such metadata and analytical results. Again, it is unrealistic to expect your users to produce such metadata and analytical tables during the workshop.
  • Users and Groups are needed to show the cooperation aspects of OMERO. You can use your production server, but this aspect is quite hard to get accross on your production server, as the User/Group setup might be very specific and users will not get a full experience of the possibilities.
